## Summary

A-Z+T v1.13.21 (main.py:16) is a Python/tkinter desktop app that drives one workflow over a single LIFT XML lexicon: collect words (optionally by recording, with ASR transcription drafts), presort them by orthography, then have a speaker SORT them by ear into groups per positional check, VERIFY each group, JOIN groups that sound the same, MACROSORT groups into alphabet glyphs, TRANSCRIBE the glyphs/tone groups, and finally run reports. The user-facing task inventory is small and gated: the chooser (tasks/chooser.py:159-201) shows exactly 3 data-collection tasks (+4 sort tasks and 1 tone-record task once ≥200 citation forms exist), 1-5 analysis tasks, and 6-13 report/export tasks. Checks are declared as a literal table in backend/core/analysis_inputs.py:614-740 — V1…V6, V1=V2…, V1xV2…, C1…C6, C1=C2…, CxV1-6, VxC1-3, the three syllable primitives (#C, C#, syls), whole-word profile checks (lc/lx/pl/imp), and a single tone check 'T' whose real dimension is user-defined tone FRAMES. Tone is the app's differentiator: surface tone is stored per frame on sense examples and composed into underlying-form (UF) categories by backend/core/analysis.py:351 sorttoUFs, which groups words sharing the same cross-frame signature and drafts names like Noun_CVC_1. Audio is first-class: per-entry PyAudio WAV recording and playback, ffmpeg decoding of phone-recorded m4a/mp3, right-click-to-open-in-Praat, and 16 kHz downsampling for ASR and for HuggingFace-style training tarballs (io_put/export.py + utilities/file_sound.py:178). ASR runs locally on CPU (torch==2.7.1+cpu) via openai-whisper, HF transformers pipelines (facebook/mms-1b-*, neurlang/ipa-whisper-base, katyayego/Wav2Vec2Phoneme-CSfinetune) and optionally allosaurus (Linux only); models are downloaded once from HuggingFace and cached, so it is offline AFTER first fetch. For Papua this matters concretely: the shipped MMS coverage table has NO adapter for Fayu (fau) or Iau (tmu), and the sister-language search finds zero ASR-supported relatives anywhere in the Lakes Plain lineage, so only the language-independent IPA/phone recognizers are usable — Indonesian (ind) is supported by both Whisper and MMS and could be selected as a "sister" for LWC material. Outputs are XLingPaper XML plus .txt reports (the XeLaTeX→PDF path exists but is gated on developer mode and commented "This isn't working yet", io_put/xlp.py:75), plus two real ReportLab PDF products: an alphabet chart and a full alphabet-comparison/minimal-pair booklet with cover, logo, contributors, imprint, prose pages and signature imposition. Data in/out is LIFT-only (plus the bundled SILCAWL LIFT wordlist template and langtags.json); there is no FLEx/WeSay live sync — interop is LIFT export/import, by design (docs/FLEX_COLLABORATION.md). It runs on Windows/Linux/macOS with Python ≥3.10, self-bootstraps a venv and pip-syncs requirements.txt on every start; there is no Android/tablet build. Localisation ships English + Arabic, Spanish, French, Lingala (DRC) and Chinese — Indonesian is NOT among them, though "Indonesian" exists as a display name for gloss languages (settings/__init__.py:1107).
